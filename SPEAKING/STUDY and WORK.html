How to use this guide

  1. Study — read a question and say your own answer aloud first. Then tap “Show sample answer” and compare: notice the phrases the speaker uses, not the whole paragraph.
  2. Flashcards — question on the front, sample answer on the back. Speak for 20–30 seconds before you flip, then check what you missed.
  3. Mark progress — tap ★ (or “Mark practised” on a flashcard) once you can answer smoothly without reading. The bar at the top remembers it, even after you close the file.
  4. My notes — open the notes box under any question and rewrite the answer about your name, your studies and your examples. It saves automatically in this browser.
  5. Key phrases — the building blocks extracted from the worksheet answers, each with its meaning and the sentence it came from. Tap 🔊 to hear any question, answer or phrase.

Exam tips for Part 1

  • Answer in 2–4 sentences: direct answer → reason → example or detail.
  • Don’t memorise full paragraphs — examiners can tell. Learn the phrases, then rebuild the answer in your own words.
  • Fillers are fine: “Well…”, “You see…”, “So I guess…”, “You know…” — the worksheet answers use them naturally.
  • Record yourself on your phone, then compare with the 🔊 audio of the sample answer.
